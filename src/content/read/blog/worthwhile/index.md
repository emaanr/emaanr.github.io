# Worthwhile

> My list of things that I think make life worth living.

1. Sharing a meal and laughing with friends on a really hot day (including complaints about how absurdly hot it is).
2. Working on a daunting problem for a while and feeling like you understand it more every day that you return to tackle it.
3. More generally, the feeling of growth and improvement.
4. Climbing.
5. Pulling off crazy plays in video games (pulling off anything really).
6. Driving, listening to music, and taking in the views on scenic routes.
7. Relaxing at home or in a coffee shop on days with precipitation (rain, snow, thunder).
8. Seeing and hearing lightning stike.
9.  Autumn leaves, crisp cold air, snow, and the holiday season.
10. Cats (and more broadly, animals, but I like cats and birds most probably).
11. Coming home, showering and getting (the outside off of you) clean (body and clothes).
12. Getting warm when it's cold and cooling off when it's hot.
13. The feeling of a familiar scent in the air that you either can't recall or can only vaguely recall the instigating memory of.
14. Watches are cool and quite beautiful in my opinion (I guess this applies broadly to artisanship, but I like watches).
15. Janky home-made solutions for specific problems and inconveniences.
16. When the thing you are excited to get finally arrives.
17. Gestures of kindness and consideration (giving and receiving!).
18. Music and art in general.
19. More specifically when the kind of song you need or want presents itself to you (hits).
20. The way my head feels after a shower (alert and refreshed).
21. Sunsets and sunrises (seriously, kinda crazy we get one of each everyday)!
22. Reminds me, I am a big "blue hour" enjoyer.
