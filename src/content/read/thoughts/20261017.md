I don’t think my past self (who was pretty lackadaisical about calculus and probably other subjects in grade school) would’ve expected maxima and minima to come up even once, let alone twice. Lately, I find myself wishing that I'd taken learning more seriously back then. My cope is that revisiting the concepts now onwards will provide a depth of understanding that I likely would not have been able to achieve back then anyway.

Side note, "lackadaisical" is quite the _word_, and "ostensibly" is _even_ funkier.
