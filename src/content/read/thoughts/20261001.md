I feel I am incorrect when I say there isn't enough time. There is often enough time for everything I'd like to do. Energy, focus, and prioritization (mainly against social maintenance) seem to be the actual bottlenecks. Claiming I lack time might suggest that resting, or "doing nothing," isn't a legitimate use of it, which is also incorrect. There is a perpetual feeling of being anywhere but present, always thinking about what I "ought" to be doing. But on what grounds and to what end? The ideal solution is to cultivate more energy and improve my ability to focus so that whatever time I don't spend resting is used efficiently. Rest is productive, but it can feel like an unconvincing claim when it's not "producing" something immediately tangible.
