> "In exchange for power, you can no longer look away from the things you don't want to see." — Yukari Takeba, _Persona 3_.

To me, knowledge is power, so this feels related to the saying that ignorance is bliss. I would further interpret that it isn't exactly that "you can no longer look away", but you can no longer _absolve accountability_ for looking away. Concisely, knowledge revokes the refuge of ignorance (being bliss). Anyway, I liked this line quite a lot.
